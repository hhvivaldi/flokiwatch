About FlokiWatch

THE STORY BEHIND THE WATCHDOG

FlokiWatch started with a simple question: can I build a system smart enough to analyze markets and make trading decisions on its own? What began as a personal experiment has grown into a full automated trading platform, and it's only getting started.

The name comes from Floki — a rescue dog from Uzbekistan with the scruffy charm of Tramp from Lady and the Tramp. He spends his days watching everything that moves from the window. FlokiWatch does the same thing, but with financial markets.

What it is

FlokiWatch is an automated trading monitoring platform. At its core, a bot analyzes markets in real time using multiple layers of intelligence — technical analysis, macroeconomic data, machine learning, momentum detection, and economic calendar events — then makes autonomous trading decisions. This dashboard is the live window into what the bot is thinking and doing, right now.

Where we are

FlokiWatch currently operates on XAU/USD (gold) in demo mode as we fine-tune and validate the system. This is our testing ground.

Where we're going

The vision is much bigger. On the markets side, we're expanding into commodities (silver, oil, natural gas), forex pairs, and crypto (BTC, ETH, and beyond).

But the real ambition is making the bot smarter. Here's what's on the roadmap: advanced sentiment analysis pulling from more news sources and social media in real time, deeper machine learning models trained on longer historical windows with more features, adaptive risk management that adjusts position sizing and stop losses based on current market regime, multi-timeframe correlation analysis to catch signals that single-timeframe analysis misses, and eventually portfolio-level intelligence — where the bot doesn't just trade one instrument, but manages a diversified portfolio with cross-asset hedging.

We're also working on better post-trade analysis tools to understand not just what the bot decided, but why — and how to make it better next time. This is a project that never stops evolving.

Who is Floki

Floki is a rescue dog from Uzbekistan with an unknown breed and a whole lot of personality. If you've seen Lady and the Tramp, picture Tramp — that scruffy, street-smart look with a heart of gold. His favorite hobby? Chasing the neighborhood cats with the same intensity we chase good trades. He never catches them, but he never stops trying — and honestly, that's the kind of persistence you want in a trading bot. Always alert, always watching, never misses a movement. Whether it's a cat crossing the garden or a candlestick crossing a moving average, Floki is on it. The perfect market watchdog.
