A dog that watches everything that moves.

FlokiWatch is an automated trading platform, named after a rescue dog who spends all day at the window watching cats cross the garden. It started as a personal experiment — can a system be smart enough to read markets on its own? — and grew from there.

§ 01 THE ORIGIN STORY

It started with a simple question.

Can I build a system smart enough to analyze markets and make trading decisions on its own?

What began as a personal experiment has grown into a full automated trading platform — a bot that reads price action, macro data, news flow and economic calendars in real time, and then decides what to do about it. This dashboard is the live window into what it's thinking.

The name comes from Floki — a rescue dog from Uzbekistan with the scruffy charm of Tramp from Lady and the Tramp. He spends his days watching everything that moves outside the window. FlokiWatch does the same thing, but with financial markets.

§ 02 WHAT IT IS

An autonomous trading monitor.

At its core, FlokiWatch is a bot that analyzes markets in real time using several layers of intelligence — technical analysis, macroeconomic data, machine learning, momentum detection, and economic calendar events — then makes autonomous trading decisions.

The dashboard you're looking at is the live window into what the bot is thinking and doing, right now. It's not a backtest. It's not a simulation. It's the system's working mind.

§ 03 WHERE WE ARE

XAU/USD, in demo. For now.

FlokiWatch currently operates on XAU/USD — gold — in demo mode as we fine-tune and validate the system. This is our testing ground. Every decision the bot makes here is real in every way except the money; that's by design, while the behavior is still being shaped.

§ 04 WHERE WE'RE GOING

The vision is much bigger.

On the markets side, we're expanding into commodities — silver, oil, natural gas — forex pairs, and crypto: BTC, ETH, and beyond.

But the real ambition is making the bot smarter. Advanced sentiment analysis pulling from more news sources and social media in real time. Deeper machine learning models trained on longer historical windows with richer features. Adaptive risk management that adjusts position sizing and stop losses based on the current market regime. Multi-timeframe correlation analysis to catch signals that single-timeframe analysis misses. And eventually portfolio-level intelligence — where the bot doesn't just trade one instrument, but manages a diversified portfolio with cross-asset hedging.

We're also working on better post-trade analysis tools — to understand not just what the bot decided, but why, and how to make it better next time.

This is a project that never stops evolving. — FLOKIWATCH ROADMAP
