§ 01 SHIPPED

The trading office is built.

17 ITEMS
FLO-001
ARCHITECTURE

8-agent autonomous architecture

Six AI agents and two deterministic agents running 24/5 — a full autonomous trading office dedicated to XAU/USD.

FLO-002
AGENT

Floki, agentic trader FLOKI

GPT-5.4 with 28 tools, self-scheduling on 5–30 minute cycles. Sole decision-maker across entry, exit, sizing, and management.

FLO-190
DEBATE

Rex bull/bear debate REX

Two opposing analysts argue BUY and SELL in parallel before every Floki cycle. Adversarial reasoning as a first-class input.

FLO-194
ORACLE

Research manager / Oracle

Gemini 3 Flash picks a winner between Rex Bull and Rex Bear and returns a verdict with entry, stop-loss, and target.

FLO-046
MACRO

Luna, macro analyst

15-minute cycles on MT5, Yahoo Finance, and FRED. Produces macro bias, risk score, and correlation tracking for Floki.

FLO-058
NEWS

Echo, news sentinel

25 RSS feeds monitored 24/7. Pull-based — Floki decides when to check. Never interrupts the desk.

FLO-072
AUDIT

Sage, daily auditor

Gemini-powered review pass at 21:00 UTC. Walks through every trade, pattern, and performance metric of the session.

FLO-080
WATCHDOG

Simba, watchdog

Zero-AI-cost 30-second polling loop. Wakes Floki on critical conditions. Pure deterministic Python.

FLO-095
REGIME

Market regime detection

Seven regimes classified continuously — volatile, quiet, breakout imminent, trending bull/bear, ranging, transitional.

FLO-085
SAFETY

Safety system & hard gates

Opposing-position hard gate, max 3 positions, 6% daily loss cap, volatility and market-hours guards — enforced before any order leaves the desk.

FLO-110
POSITIONS

Floki-owned position management

Breakeven moves, trailing stops, and drawdown phases live inside Floki. The EA is a pure executor — no hidden logic on the MT5 side.

FLO-125
UI

Trade Room dashboard redesign

Bloomberg-style terminal. All seven agents visible. Command strip, position bar, deep-dive panels — one screen, no chrome.

FLO-060
EXECUTION

EA bridge execution

JSON signals plus tick-by-tick EA management. Python writes the signal; the MQL5 EA places the order on MetaTrader 5.

FLO-130
MEMORY

Reflexion engine & semantic memory

ChromaDB vector store with enriched post-trade embeddings. Floki can search his own history by meaning, not just keyword.

FLO-165
CHARTING

S/R zones on the MT5 chart

Bridge pattern — Python writes JSON, the EA draws the zones. H1 intraday layered over H4 structural levels.

FLO-211
SCANNER

Rex Monitor, zero-LLM scanner REX

Background analyst that runs four deterministic checks every 30 minutes: divergence, correlation, regime history, session performance.

FLO-218
UI

Watch-condition display

Human-readable watch conditions with colored arrows. No technical IDs — a trader can read the desk at a glance.
